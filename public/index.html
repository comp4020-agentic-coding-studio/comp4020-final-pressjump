What it does

Modelling, built for more than one person.

Bench is 3D CAD where everyone in the design sees the same part at the same moment, and nobody's work disappears without them seeing it.

Start a design →

Free · Browser · Phone · STL

See everyone, live

A name and a colour on every cursor and on whatever each person holds. Drafts show before anyone lets go.

Nobody gets overwritten

Every edit is checked against the version it was made on. If someone got there first, you're told who.

Numbers you can trust

Millimetres and degrees to three decimals. Every dimension of every feature can be typed in.

One shared timeline

Boxes, cylinders, sketches, extrudes, joins and cuts in the order they were made. Scrub back through it.

Phone to desktop

Orbit with one finger, pan and zoom with two. Every command is in reach at phone width.

Straight to the printer

The STL is built on the server from the saved design, so the file you print is the part you agreed on.

How it works

One link, one part, everyone on it at once.

Share the link, and they're in

No accounts and no installs. Whoever opens it joins the room with their own name and colour.

Work side by side without stepping on each other

A body someone holds is theirs until they let go. If your change is refused, Bench says why and who.
